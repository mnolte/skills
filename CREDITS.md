Special thanks to [Matt Pocock](https://github.com/mattpocock) for being a highly inspirational and influential figure in the development of this repository.

His public work on teaching, open-source tooling and practical workflows for AI-assisted development shaped how these skills think about working with coding agents: ask sharper questions up front, keep written context small and precise, and treat the agent as a collaborator that needs clear direction.
